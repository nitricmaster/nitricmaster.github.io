About Me

I am a Master of Financial Engineering student at the UCLA Anderson School of Management (expected December 2027). I hold an M.S. in Applied Economics from the University of Maryland and a B.S. in Economics from Wenzhou-Kean University.

My work combines quantitative finance and econometrics. Recent projects range from Black–Scholes pricing, implied volatility and delta-hedging backtests built from scratch in Python, to a staggered difference-in-differences study of whether Uber reduces drunk driving.

Previously, I interned in the Personal Loan Department of the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, where I built regression models of how personal loan demand responds to interest-rate changes.
