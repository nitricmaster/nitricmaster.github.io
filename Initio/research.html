Stochastic Calculus in Practice: Option Pricing & Hedging in Python

Nov 2026

Objective: Applied continuous-time finance — geometric Brownian motion, Black–Scholes, and delta hedging — to real market data.

  • Calibrated GBM to 5 years of daily data on a Shanghai-listed stock (vol 35.7% ± 0.7%, drift statistically insignificant).
  • Built Black–Scholes pricing, Greeks and an implied-volatility solver from scratch for SSE 50ETF options.
  • Backtested daily delta hedging of ~1,100 short ATM calls; hedging P&L correlated 0.88 with the gap between pricing and realized volatility.
  • Replicated a protective put in 100-share lots, cutting the worst 60-day loss from 6.3% to 3.4%.

Does Uber Reduce Drunk Driving? A Staggered Difference-in-Differences Analysis

Jan 2026 – May 2026

Objective: Estimated whether Uber's staggered entry into U.S. cities reduced drunk-driving fatalities.

  • Constructed a 7,194-observation city-year panel combining NHTSA FARS DUI-fatality data, Census population data, and staggered Uber entry dates; defined DUI fatalities per 100,000 residents and treatment timing.
  • Estimated two-way fixed-effects difference-in-differences models with city and year fixed effects.
  • Assessed identification using an event study, placebo tests, sample-window robustness checks, and a Goodman-Bacon decomposition.

ICT, Productivity, and U.S. Economic Growth: A Python Panel Analysis

Sep 2023 – Dec 2023

Objective: Investigated how the diffusion of ICT influences U.S. economic development and productivity growth.

  • Built a panel dataset covering 50 U.S. states from 2015–2022 using Census and BEA data on ICT, income, and demographics.
  • Estimated two-way fixed-effects models in Python and visualized results to evaluate the relationship between digitalization and real GDP growth.

Green Credit Policy and Environmental Pollution in China: A Python Panel Analysis

Sep 2023 – Dec 2023

Objective: Assessed the impact of China's green credit policies on reducing industrial air pollution and promoting sustainability.

  • Integrated provincial economic, financial, energy, and environmental data for China from 2000–2020 and constructed a Green Credit Index.
  • Applied two-way fixed-effects regression in Python and performed multicollinearity and model-consistency checks to assess pollution-mitigation effects.
